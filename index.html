Who I work with.

Learn-to-Swim (all ages)

From water familiarisation through to confident freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly. I work with children and adults at all stages - whether it's your first time in the pool or you're refining technique after years away.

Development & Competitive Squads

Training swimmers who are moving into club competition or already racing at state and national level. Focus on building proper stroke mechanics, race strategy, and the mental side of performance under pressure.

Adult Fitness Swimmers

Structured sessions for adults training for fitness, triathlons, or ocean swims. Emphasis on sustainable technique, injury prevention, and progressive training loads that fit around work and life.
